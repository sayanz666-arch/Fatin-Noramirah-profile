01 — About

A working operator, not a presenter.

The work I'm proudest of doesn't make slide decks. It shows up in OTA performance reports that hit target, repeat bookings from corporate clients, and the quiet shift in service quality when a complaint is handled before it becomes a review.

I started in tourism — riding the Penang hop-on bus telling tourists about heritage shophouses, learning that sales is really just storytelling that converts. From there: bank debt recovery (where I learned negotiation under pressure), logistics admin (the spreadsheet years), and then four years inside J&T Express where I was first-batch hire for two new departments — Customer Service and VIP Relationship — and got to build playbooks from a blank page.

Today at Farm In The City, I own the commercial relationships that drive ticket sales and venue bookings. OTA management, partnership campaigns with banks and hotels, private event coordination at D'Pine Log House, and the reporting that ties it all together.

I'm strongest where strategy meets execution detail — and I prefer environments where the brief changes by lunchtime.
